# Language and Direction Test Plan

## Application Overview

The site is bilingual: Persian (default, right-to-left) and English (left-to-right). Every URL carries
the locale (`/fa/...`, `/en/...`); `/` sends visitors to their last chosen language or Persian. The
choice is remembered in a cookie. Unknown pages show a localized 404. Keyboard users get a skip link.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Language and direction

**Seed:** `e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1. persian-is-the-default

**File:** `e2e/i18n/persian-is-the-default.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/` - expect: the URL is `/fa` - expect: `<html>` has `dir="rtl"` and `lang="fa"` - expect: the main heading is the Persian home title

#### 1.2. switching-language-is-remembered

**File:** `e2e/i18n/switching-language-is-remembered.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/fa` and press the language switcher ("English") - expect: the URL is `/en`, `<html dir="ltr">`, the heading is the English home title
2. Open `/` again - expect: the URL is `/en` (the choice was remembered)

#### 1.3. unknown-page-shows-localized-404

**File:** `e2e/i18n/unknown-page-shows-localized-404.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/fa/does-not-exist` - expect: HTTP status 404 - expect: the heading is the Persian "page not found" title

#### 1.4. skip-link-for-keyboard-users

**File:** `e2e/i18n/skip-link-for-keyboard-users.spec.ts`

**Steps:**

1. Open `/en` and press Tab once - expect: the "Skip to content" link is focused (Chromium only; WebKit doesn't Tab-focus links by default)
