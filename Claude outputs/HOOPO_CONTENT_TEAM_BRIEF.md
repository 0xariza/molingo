## 1. What Hoopo is

**Hoopo (هوپو, hoopo.ir) is an online home for independent language teachers who run small live classes.** A teacher manages her classes, schedule and learners in one place, and her learners join class from their phone with one tap. Hoopo is built and hosted in Iran, so it's designed for Iranian mobile networks and keeps running when the international internet is cut.

- **Every teacher gets her own branded page**, e.g. "Mona English" at `hoopo.ir/mona`. Hoopo stays in the background as a small "powered by hoopo". **Hoopo is never the teacher's brand.**
- **Today:** one teacher, Mona, runs her real English classes on Hoopo. Multi-teacher support is built, and new teachers join **by invitation only**.
- **Next:** Mona's colleagues, then any language teacher.
- **Long term:** Hoopo also becomes an **AI companion** that stands beside both the learner and the teacher. It isn't built yet, so it's always "coming soon".

**Tagline**

- فارسی: **«تدریس با شما، بقیه‌اش با هوپو.»**
- English: **"You teach. Hoopo handles the rest."**

### The core promise (the idea behind every piece of content)

**The teacher only teaches.** She works inside an efficient system and carries no worry about admin work or managing her learners: Hoopo handles that.

- فارسی: **معلم فقط تدریس می‌کند؛ در یک سیستم کارآمد، بدون هیچ دغدغه‌ای برای کارهای اداری و مدیریت زبان‌آموزها.**
- English: **The teacher just teaches, inside an efficient system, with no worries about admin work or managing learners.**

Every post should make this promise feel real by showing **one concrete piece of admin that disappears**.

| Admin the teacher no longer worries about           | What Hoopo does instead                                                           | Status         |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Scheduling, rescheduling, make-up sessions          | Persian-date weekly schedule; changes take a few taps and every learner sees them | ✅ Today       |
| "What time is class?" / "Send me the link" messages | Learners see their own schedule and join with one tap                             | ✅ Today       |
| Keeping track of learners and groups                | All learners, classes and enrolments in one place                                 | ✅ Today       |
| Knowing where each learner stands                   | Personal A1–C2 path, practice, end-of-level exams                                 | ✅ Today       |
| Booking placement calls with new learners           | Learners book a free level check themselves from the teacher's page               | ✅ Today       |
| Tuition, invoices, payment reminders                | Handled automatically                                                             | 🟡 Coming soon |

> ⚠️ **Claim discipline:** "no worries about admin" is the **direction** of the product, not a guarantee. Tuition and payments aren't automated yet. So never write "Hoopo handles **all** your admin" or "you'll never deal with payments again". Write the promise as above, and back each post with a ✅ example from the table.

## 2. The learning journey: the heart of Hoopo (available today)

Hoopo is more than a tool for running classes. Its core idea is a **clear, personal learning path for every learner**:

1. **The teacher maps each learner's path** from A1 to C2, in small stages with concrete goals.
2. **Everyone knows exactly where the learner is.** Teacher and learner can see the current stop, what's done and what comes next.
3. **Continuous practice** between classes, tied to the learner's own stage.
4. **An exam at the end of each level.** Passing it moves the learner to the next level.

- فارسی: معلم برای هر زبان‌آموز مسیر پیشرفت خودش را ترسیم می‌کند و همیشه می‌داند او دقیقاً کجای راه است؛ زبان‌آموز تمرین مستمر دارد و در پایان هر سطح آزمون می‌دهد.
- English: The teacher maps every learner's path and always knows exactly where they are; learners practise continuously and take an exam at the end of each level.

**The AI (coming soon):** Hoopo the AI will be a personal companion beside both people. For the learner, it's a practice partner between classes that knows their path. For the teacher, it's an assistant that shows what each learner needs before the next class. **The AI never replaces the teacher.**

## 3. The problem we solve

Independent teachers in Iran run a real business across scattered tools:

- **The schedule** lives in a notebook or spreadsheet, and every change means another round of chat messages.
- **The class** runs on video tools that stutter on mobile data or need a VPN.
- **Learner details and progress** are buried in WhatsApp and Telegram chats.
- **Tuition** is tracked by hand, and payment reminders feel awkward.
- **New learners** come by word of mouth, there's no professional page, and booking a placement call takes a dozen messages.

The result: hours of admin every week that isn't teaching.

> Describe these problems warmly, as the teacher's everyday reality. **Never name or criticise other schools, apps or teachers.**

## 7. Voice and tone

Hoopo sounds like **a capable, calm colleague** who respects teachers and takes care of the boring parts. It's not hype and it's not corporate.

- **Outcomes, not features:** "your learners see their own schedule", not "Jalali calendar module".
- **Persian first.** Write formal written Persian (فارسی نوشتاری رسمی) and address teachers as «شما». Keep it warm, not stiff, with no Finglish and no unnecessary English words.
- **Adapt the English version** from the Persian; don't translate it word for word. English is optional for posts.
- **Persian posts use Persian digits**, Persian (Jalali) dates and Toman.
- **Be positive and generous.** Never run down competitors. Present internet problems as something we've solved, not as fear.
- **Be honest.** Say "coming soon" plainly.

| ✅ Do                                                      | ⛔ Don't                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| «زبان‌آموزهایتان با یک لمس وارد کلاس می‌شوند.»             | «بهترین پلتفرم آموزش آنلاین ایران!»                |
| "Hoopo handles the schedule so you can focus on teaching." | "Revolutionary AI-powered all-in-one solution!"    |
| "You know exactly where every learner is."                 | "Track tuition automatically" (not built)          |
| "Hosted in Iran."                                          | "When the internet gets cut, everyone else fails." |

**Learner-facing tone** (on teachers' pages only): friendly and encouraging, still «شما», short sentences.

  - Closed: thinking, a tip, a question
